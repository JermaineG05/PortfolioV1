About Us

Study Buddy is a small community for students who learn better together. Everyone has something they are stuck on and something they are good at, so we make it easy to find the person whose strengths match your gaps.

Create a profile with your year level, major and main subject, list the topics you need help with and the ones you can teach, and tell us which programming languages you work in. Other students can then find you, and you can find them.

We started as a class case study with a simple belief: explaining something to a friend is one of the best ways to learn it yourself. Whether you are wrestling with JavaScript, Python or a maths assignment, there is probably a buddy who has been there.

How it works

  1. Register with your email and fill in your profile.
  2. Sign in and open the Buddies page.
  3. Filter by year level, subject or programming language.
  4. Request a buddy whose strengths match what you need.
