Time’s Up

A minigame collection for Android, built in Kotlin. You play through a variety of games, each with a time limit. When the time runs out, you move on to the next game, until you’ve played them all. The goal is to score as high as you can.

  • Flappy Bird
  • Tetris
  • Snake
  • + a couple more

  2. 2Time’s up, the next game starts
  3. 3Chase the highest score

Inventory Management System

An inventory tracker built in Excel. Shipments and sales are typed in on their own sheets, and formulas work out current stock, stock value, profit and reorder warnings for each product. A dashboard sums it all up with totals and a colour-coded stock status.

  • 5 sheets
  • 6 products
  • 240+ formulas
  • Conditional formatting
  • Reorder alerts
